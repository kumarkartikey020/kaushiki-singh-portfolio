Forensic Biology & Serology PROJECT 01

Rapid Field Blood Grouping Kit for Preliminary Crime Scene Analysis

This project focuses on developing an innovative forensic biology solution for rapid preliminary ABO blood group screening at crime scenes. The proposed concept explores antigen-antibody agglutination reactions as a means of supporting preliminary blood group identification during early stages of forensic investigation, accelerating preliminary analysis while preserving biological sample integrity for confirmatory laboratory testing.

Scientific Concepts Involved
Forensic Biology Forensic Serology ABO Blood Grouping Antigen-Antibody Agglutination Immunochromatographic Assay Crime Scene Protocols
Project Objectives
  • Explore rapid preliminary ABO blood group screening at crime scenes
  • Apply antigen-antibody agglutination principles for swift visual identification
  • Support early evidentiary assessment during critical initial investigative hours
  • Ensure biological sample preservation for subsequent confirmatory laboratory DNA analysis

Scientific Significance: Explores the potential of rapid preliminary screening methods in forensic investigations, demonstrating how practical forensic biology solutions can improve early investigative workflows without compromising downstream genetic analyses.

Status: Engineered Project Concept / Proposal
Analytical Chemistry & Quality PROJECT 02

Examination of Adulteration in Local Vendors' Milk by Physical and Chemical Methods

This academic project involved a comprehensive analytical study to assess the quality and authenticity of milk collected from local vendors using standardized physical and chemical examination techniques. The study evaluated milk quality through analytical parameters and identified possible indications of adulteration using laboratory-based testing methods.

Scientific Concepts Involved
Food Chemistry Milk Quality Analysis Physical Examination Methods Chemical Analysis Adulteration Detection pH Analysis Lactometer Test Specific Gravity Fat Content Analysis
Project Objectives
  • Assess the quality of milk samples collected from diverse local vendors
  • Examine physical and chemical characteristics against reference standards
  • Apply calibrated pH analysis, specific gravity measurement, and lactometer testing
  • Evaluate fat content and chemical parameters to investigate adulteration

Scientific Significance: Demonstrates the application of analytical chemistry and quality assessment methods to real-world food safety concerns, highlighting the importance of accurate laboratory testing, scientific documentation, and evidence-based interpretation.
